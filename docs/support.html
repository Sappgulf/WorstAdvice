Bad ideas. Great delivery.

Badvice is a satire app for entertainment. Do not follow its deliberately bad advice.

Get help

Email badvice.app@gmail.com for help, feedback, content reports, or questions about shared data. Include your app version, device model, and a description of what happened. Never send your password or sensitive situation text.

Advice without a connection

Select the Bureau Engine to use bundled local generation. Apple Intelligence requires a supported device and availability in system settings; Badvice explains when it falls back to the local engine.

Accounts and saved advice

Guest mode and protected Badvice accounts are stored on this device. They are separate from your Apple Account. Local history does not automatically sync across devices. Manage local accounts in Settings.

iCloud and Party

Optional social features use iCloud. Online Party uses Game Center; pass-the-phone play works locally. Check your Apple Account, Game Center sign-in, and network connection if online features are unavailable. Use report and block controls for unwanted social content.
