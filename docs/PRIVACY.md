# Badvice Privacy Policy

Updated November 17, 2026.

## On this device

Advice history, saved takes, preferences, progression, and local accounts are stored on this device. They do not automatically sync across devices. Protected-account credentials use the system Keychain. A local Badvice account is not an Apple ID.

## On-device generation

The offline Bureau Engine runs locally. When available and selected, Apple Intelligence generates advice on-device. Badvice does not send your situation text to an external AI service for generation.

## Optional social features

Connecting an iCloud social profile stores your display name, handle, identifiers, and content you choose to share in CloudKit. This uses a public database: a friends-only feed filter does not make the underlying records private. Do not share sensitive information.

## Online Party

Online matches use Apple's Game Center. Player identities, submitted takes, votes, and round results are shared with match participants and stored with the match. Pass-the-phone play remains local.

## Your choices

You can use Badvice as a guest, keep advice local, and decline optional notification permissions. Manage saved advice and local accounts in the app. Deleting a local account does not automatically remove previously shared CloudKit content or Game Center matches. To delete your social profile and its shared content, open Settings, iCloud & friends, then Delete iCloud social profile. Safety reports and content saved by other people may remain. For questions or help with shared data, contact badvice.app@gmail.com.

## Tracking

Badvice does not use advertising tracking SDKs. Apple operates iCloud and Game Center under its own privacy terms.
