# Badvice Support

Bad ideas. Great delivery.

Badvice is satire for entertainment. Do not follow its deliberately bad advice.

## Contact

Email [badvice.app@gmail.com](mailto:badvice.app@gmail.com) for help, feedback, content reports, or questions about shared data. Include your app version, device model, and a description of what happened. Never send your password or sensitive situation text.

## Offline advice

Select the Bureau Engine to use bundled local generation. Apple Intelligence requires a supported device and availability in system settings; Badvice explains when it falls back to the local engine.

## Accounts and saved advice

Guest mode and protected Badvice accounts are stored on this device, separately from your Apple Account. Local history does not automatically sync across devices. Manage local accounts in Settings.

To remove your iCloud social profile and its shared content, open Settings, iCloud & friends, then Delete iCloud social profile. This leaves local advice intact. Content saved by other people and safety reports may remain.

## iCloud and Party

Optional social features use iCloud. Online Party uses Game Center; pass-the-phone play works locally. Check your Apple Account, Game Center sign-in, and network connection if online features are unavailable. Use the report and block controls for unwanted social content, or contact support.
